## Generation prompts

### restaurant

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: widescreen hero photograph for Scope, a premium Scandinavian restaurant technology company. Create one exceptional realistic commercial architectural photograph, landscape 16:9, high resolution. A quietly sophisticated independent Copenhagen/Oslo bistro just before evening service, no people. CAMERA: eye-level editorial architectural photography with a wide 28mm lens, intentional asymmetric composition. Left third is a deep shadowed charcoal plaster wall and dark walnut structural pillar, clean negative space for a white editorial headline to be added separately. Middle and right show richly detailed dining room: a long caramel leather banquette, a few circular travertine tables meticulously set with linen napkins, clear wine glasses and restrained table lamps, bent wood chairs, a beautiful softly glowing oval paper pendant at the upper right, arched deep window recesses, very subtle brushed stainless steel at far back. A striking shaft of low amber evening sun crosses the right hand tables and catches the glassware; shadowed walls retain cool blue-grey depth. Dramatic contrast, genuine photographic material texture, tactile dark walnut and leather, absolutely convincing spatial geometry, no generic hotel lobby. Beautiful warm pools of light, cinematic but believable, restrained fine film grain, premium hospitality editorial from a design magazine, expensive art direction. The lower foreground table edge creates depth. Image must remain rich enough to look impressive as a full screen website background, not over-darkened into black. No text, no logos, no charts or graphical overlays, no people, no plants filling the frame, no oversaturated orange, no synthetic CGI sheen.

### dish

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial restaurant photograph for Scope, premium Scandinavian restaurant management software. Create one cinematic realistic high-end food photography image in landscape 4:3. Close overhead three-quarter composition of a beautifully but naturally plated authentic pasta carbonara on a large matte warm ivory shallow ceramic bowl, on a deep charcoal-green dark stone restaurant table. The bowl occupies right-center, confidently off-center with the left quarter mostly empty charcoal stone, a silver fork and loosely folded oatmeal linen cloth near upper left. Pasta has silky egg sauce, subtle pecorino, cracked black pepper, a few small crisp guanciale pieces, no cream pools, no elaborate garnish. Strong warm directional window light from upper right highlights the pale bowl and pasta against deeply shadowed moody tabletop. Natural imperfection and tactility, small glints on silver, real ceramic pores and stone grain. Tight luxury European bistro editorial photography, 50mm camera, extremely believable and delicious rather than polished fake food. High contrast, quietly expensive, warm highlights and slate shadows, restrained colors. No people, hands, text, branding, charts, illustrations, generative glitches or generic stock photography styling. The photographic asset itself should have no UI; website will add typography separately.
